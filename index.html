Gig Recorder Support Privacy Policy

Gig Recorder

Record the whole gig. Relive every gig as a playlist.

Gig Recorder records your entire set on iPhone, iPad, or Mac, using the built-in mic or a connected USB interface or the venue's sound board, splits the recording into songs, names them, and turns the night into a playlist you can replay and share with the band. Add a photo to any gig and it follows the recording into Playlists and Now Playing. Everything you need to record, split, name, edit, Enhance, and build playlists is free. Gig Recorder Pro (one-time purchase) adds sharing, export, Dropbox linking, iCloud sync, and the Effects screen.

Support

Include your device model, iOS or macOS version, and the app build number from Settings > About. We usually reply within two business days.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to buy anything to record?

No. Recording, splitting, naming, editing, one-tap Enhance, and playlists are free with no time limit. Pro unlocks sharing, exporting, Dropbox and iCloud sync, and Effects.

Can I record from a mixer or an audio interface?

Yes. Use the built-in mic, or connect a USB interface or the venue's sound board and pick it as your input, on iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

My bandmate sent me a shared playlist. Do I need Pro to listen?

No. Receiving and listening to a shared playlist is free. Only the person sharing needs Pro.

I bought Pro on one device. How do I get it on another?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in Gig Recorder, and tap Restore Purchase. Pro also supports Family Sharing.

Where are my recordings stored?

On your device, inside the app's own folder. They are never uploaded unless you turn on iCloud features, connect Dropbox, or share something. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up my recordings?

Turn on iCloud session sync in Settings (Pro) to mirror your editable gigs to iCloud Drive, or export a gig to an iCloud Drive or Dropbox folder. Deleting the app deletes recordings that were only on the device, so export or sync anything you want to keep.

The recording stopped or the app was killed mid-set. What happened?

Keep the device plugged in, keep Gig Recorder in the foreground or locked with the app still running, and avoid switching to other audio apps while recording. If you use a USB interface, connect it before you press Record. If a recording was cut short, the part already captured is saved.

Songs were not split or named correctly.

Open the set in the Song Editor to move, add, or delete markers and rename songs. Naming improves over time as the app learns your songs from the recordings you name.

Can I add a photo to a gig?

Yes. Add a photo to any gig and it shows up in Playlists, shared playlists, and Now Playing, so you can spot the right night at a glance.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the Gig Recorder Pro purchase.

How do I delete my data?

Delete gigs or playlists inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything on the device. Files you exported to iCloud Drive or Dropbox are yours to delete from those services. We hold no copies of anything.
